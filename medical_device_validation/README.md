## Quick Reference: Key Standards

```
ISO 13485:2016  → QMS for medical device organisations
ISO 14971:2019  → Risk management for medical devices
21 CFR Part 11  → FDA rule for electronic records & signatures
EU MDR 2017/745 → EU Medical Device Regulation (replaced MDD)
IEC 60601-1     → General safety/performance for electrical medical devices
IEC 62304:2006+AMD1:2015 → Medical device software lifecycle processes
IEC 62366-1     → Usability engineering / human factors
21 CFR Part 820 → FDA Quality System Regulation (replaced by QMSR 2024)
```
